Uneven-Terrain VTOL Landing System

Independent research · Project 1203X12

Helicopters and drones that land on slopes or debris risk dynamic rollover or propeller strikes — a real problem for search-and-rescue, disaster relief, and urban air mobility, where a flat pad isn't guaranteed. Most existing fixes rely on LiDAR or mid-air adjustment, both of which fail when sensors get noisy or shifts happen after touchdown.

I designed a passive, mechanical landing gear that locks on impact rather than adjusting mid-flight — legs modeled after push-button mechanisms in umbrellas, 3D-printed in carbon fiber, with a spring-loaded pin per leg. Each configuration was CFD-tested in SimScale to check drag and aerodynamic disturbance before being built and flight-tested outdoors.

Analyze Sports — Live Shot-Data Visualization

Team project (5) · McAlister Sec. 017 · for Computers for Kids (C4K)

+ add photo — app screenshot or live heatmap
+ add photo — tabletop basketball setup

Built for Computers for Kids, a non-profit teaching data science to 11–14 year-olds through hands-on workshops. Their tabletop-basketball activity used manual Google Forms entry after every shot, which interrupted gameplay and put up a barrier for younger, less tech-savvy players.

Our team replaced it with a browser-based app (Django/Python) that logs each make or miss by court zone in real time, generating a live shooting-percentage heatmap as the game is played — turning the data collection itself into part of the activity instead of a pause in it.
